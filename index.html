lamoon-p

Small tools that run entirely in your browser.

  • QR
    Paste a link, get a QR code. Client-side only.
  • HEIC → JPG
    Convert HEIC to JPG in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • A8 on A4
    Tile A4 PDFs 16-up onto printable A4 sheets. Client-side only.
  • Base64
    Encode text to base64 and back. Client-side only.
